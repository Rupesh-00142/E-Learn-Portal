Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This page explains what information E-Learn collects, why, and how it's kept.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored securely as a hash — we never store or can see your actual password). You can optionally add a profile photo.

When you enroll in a course, we record which course and your progress. If you're an Admin, any course details and files you add are stored as part of the platform's content.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account,
  • To show you the courses you've enrolled in and your progress,
  • To let Admins manage the courses and files on the platform,
  • To respond to messages sent through the Contact page.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

E-Learn stores a login session token in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in between visits. This token is used only to keep you signed in and is removed when you log out.

4. How Your Data Is Stored

Account and course data is stored in our database on the server that runs E-Learn. Uploaded files (PDFs, PPTs, etc.) are stored on that same server. Passwords are hashed before storage — they are never saved as plain text.

5. Your Choices

You can update your name and profile photo at any time from your Profile page. If you'd like your account and personal data deleted, contact us through the Contact page and we'll process your request.

6. Children's Privacy

E-Learn is intended for school, college, and university students. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through our Contact page.
