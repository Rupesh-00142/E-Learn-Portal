Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using E-Learn.

Is E-Learn free to use? Signing up and browsing notes is completely free. Some notes may have a price set by the admin, shown on the course page before you enroll.
How do I access a course's notes? Find a course on the Courses page, open it, and click "Enroll Now." Once enrolled, you can view the full lesson content and download any attached files (PDF, PPT, DOC) from your Profile page.
Can I download the notes to read offline? Yes. If a module has an attached file, you'll see a download button on the lesson page once you're enrolled. Files include PDFs, PowerPoint slides, Word documents, and text files.
Who adds the notes on this platform? Notes are added by our Admin and Super Admin accounts. If you'd like to contribute notes, reach out through the Contact page.
I forgot my password. What do I do? Use the "Forgot password?" link on the Log In page, or reach out through the Contact page and we'll help you regain access.
Can I use E-Learn on my phone? Yes, the whole site works on mobile browsers — no app download needed.
Still have a question? Visit our Contact page and send us a message — we typically reply within 24 hours.
